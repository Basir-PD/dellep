# Dellep

Dellep is a one-person Meta ads service for roofing companies. The site exists to do one thing: turn a roofer who has been contacted through cold outreach into a booked call about a free 30-day ads pilot.

## Language

### People and markets

**Roofer**:
The owner or decision-maker of a roofing company — the only person Dellep sells to.
_Avoid_: client, customer, lead, business owner

**Prospect**:
A Roofer who has completed an Intake but has not yet started a Pilot.
_Avoid_: lead, contact, submission

**Pilot Client**:
A Roofer whose Pilot has started, meaning Account Access has been granted.
_Avoid_: client, account

**Served Market**:
The regions Dellep sells into: the United States, and Quebec, Canada. A Roofer outside these is Not A Fit regardless of budget.
_Avoid_: territory, region, geo

### The offer

**Pilot**:
A fixed 30-day engagement in which Dellep manages a Roofer's Meta ads at no charge, in exchange for Account Access, closed-loop feedback, and Case Study Rights.
_Avoid_: trial, free trial, test, engagement, project

**Ad Spend Budget**:
The monthly amount a Roofer pays **Meta** to run their ads. It is never money paid to Dellep — during a Pilot, Dellep charges nothing.
_Avoid_: investment, budget, spend, marketing budget, fee

**Ad Spend Floor**:
The minimum monthly Ad Spend Budget below which a Pilot cannot produce a meaningful result, so the Roofer is Not A Fit.
_Avoid_: minimum, threshold, qualification

**Account Access**:
Admin permission on the Roofer's Meta Business Manager and Facebook Page. A Pilot has not started until this is granted.
_Avoid_: access, permissions, credentials, login

**Closed Loop**:
The Roofer reporting back which generated leads booked an inspection and which became signed jobs. Without it a Pilot yields a cost-per-lead number but no Case Study.
_Avoid_: reporting, feedback, attribution

**Case Study**:
The published record of a completed Pilot's numbers, used as proof to win the next Roofer. It is the sole compensation Dellep receives for a Pilot.
_Avoid_: testimonial, results, portfolio piece, social proof

**Case Study Rights**:
The Roofer's up-front permission to publish their Pilot's numbers and provide a short testimonial.

### The funnel

**Intake**:
The set of qualifying questions a Roofer answers before being offered a Booking.
_Avoid_: questionnaire, form, survey, application, assessment

**Qualified**:
An Intake outcome meaning the Roofer is in a Served Market and meets the Ad Spend Floor. Only a Qualified Roofer is shown the Booking step.
_Avoid_: accepted, approved, passed

**Not A Fit**:
An Intake outcome meaning no Booking is offered. The Roofer's contact details are still captured, since circumstances change.
_Avoid_: rejected, disqualified, failed, unqualified

**Booking**:
A scheduled call between a Qualified Roofer and Dellep, held on the Roofer's own timezone.
_Avoid_: appointment, meeting, calendar, demo, consultation
