# Free 30-day Pilot as the entry offer

Dellep has no case study, no repeatable process, and no ability to promise an outcome, so the page cannot use the standard agency offer. We decided the entry offer is a **free** 30-day Pilot: Dellep manages the Roofer's Meta ads at no charge, and the Roofer pays only their own Ad Spend Budget to Meta. The purpose of the first Pilot is not revenue — it is to manufacture the Case Study that makes every later Roofer easier to win.

## Considered Options

A low paid pilot (~$500/month, month-to-month, cancel anytime) was recommended and rejected in favour of free. The argument for charging was that money buys commitment: paying Roofers grant Account Access promptly, answer questions, and close the Closed Loop, whereas free Roofers deprioritise the work and drift off — leaving Dellep with no Case Study, which was the entire point.

## Consequences

- Because free carries this risk, the Pilot is structured as an **exchange, not a favour**. The Roofer pays in Account Access granted before day one, Closed Loop feedback, and Case Study Rights agreed up front. These terms are the price, and they are non-negotiable in a way a free favour could not be.
- The Pilot has a fixed 30-day end date so there is a natural moment to convert to a paid engagement. An open-ended free arrangement never converts.
- This price cannot sustain a business. It is correct only until the first Case Study exists, at which point it should be retired.
