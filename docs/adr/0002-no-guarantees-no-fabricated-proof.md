# No outcome guarantees and no fabricated social proof

Dellep cannot honestly promise a result yet, so the site makes no outcome claims — no lead counts, no revenue figures, no "or you don't pay" guarantee — and displays no testimonials, client logos, or numbers that are not real. Credibility is carried instead by the risk-free shape of the Pilot and by a detailed, roofing-specific account of the work that will actually be done in 30 days.

## Context

The site previously shipped fabricated proof inherited from a template: invented practitioners with Unsplash stock photos as headshots in `constants/testimonials.ts`, and a logo marquee implying Netflix, Google, Meta and OnlyFans were clients. Both were removed. The previous headline, "10-20+ New Patients Every Month Or You Don't Pay", was an outcome guarantee that could not be honoured.

## Consequences

- A future contributor will notice the page has no social proof section and may try to add one. **This is deliberate.** Nothing goes on the page that Dellep cannot evidence on a call.
- Specificity substitutes for proof. A Roofer cannot evaluate results he has not seen, but he can tell within seconds whether the page understands storm-damage angles, service-radius targeting, and what a realistic cost-per-lead looks like in his trade. That section is the credibility mechanism and must not be diluted into generic agency copy.
- The first real Case Study replaces this posture. Adding genuine proof to an honest page is easy; retracting a claim already made is not.
