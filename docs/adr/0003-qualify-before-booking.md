# Qualify before Booking, and turn away Roofers below the Ad Spend Floor

A Roofer must complete the Intake before any Booking slots are shown, and a Roofer whose Ad Spend Budget falls below the Ad Spend Floor is marked Not A Fit and shown no calendar at all. The Ad Spend Floor is set at **$1,000/month as a hard gate**, with $1,500/month as the level a Pilot actually wants.

## Why

Meta needs roughly 50 conversions per week to leave the learning phase. Below about $1,500/month a Pilot generates noise rather than a result, so a Roofer with a small budget costs Dellep exactly as much time as a good one and produces no Case Study. Dellep also runs this around a full-time job, so call slots are the scarcest resource on the project and must not be spent on Roofers who cannot produce the one asset the Pilot exists to create.

## Consequences

- **Deliberately turning away traffic looks like a bug.** It is not. A future contributor should not "fix" the gate by showing the calendar to everyone.
- Contact details are captured on the Intake step **before** the calendar, so an abandoned or Not A Fit Intake still yields a Prospect worth following up later.
- Placing questions ahead of the calendar lowers raw booking rate and raises show rate, which is the correct trade when the constraint is Dellep's time rather than traffic volume.
